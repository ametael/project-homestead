# Knowledge

The template starts with an empty method catalog. Use [Knowledge and reusable methods](../OPERATIONS/KNOWLEDGE.md) to create or find entries in your local `MEMORY/KNOWLEDGE/INDEX.md`.

Two kinds of card share one index. A [method card](../TEMPLATES/METHOD.md) records an approach that worked. A [failure card](../TEMPLATES/FAILURE.md) records one that did not, under a `fail.` ID, with the replacement and the recheck trigger. Check failures first when a symptom looks familiar.

A card carries one status: `VERIFIED`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `FAILED`, `STALE` or `SUPERSEDED`. Keep one card per ID and update it instead of adding a near duplicate.

[Class and skill catalog](CLASSES.md) defines progression categories independently of saved methods and earned progress.
